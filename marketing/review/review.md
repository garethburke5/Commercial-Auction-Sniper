## 9. Facebook

Four city-centre shops in Colchester, with a £1.1m guide and £136,400 advertised annual rent.

The freehold at 6–7 High Street, 1–4 Bank Passage and 5A Culver Street West includes a jewellery shop let to EH (Colchester) Limited, trading as Emson Haig, at £80,000 a year. The other three commercial lettings contribute £56,400. Together, that produces a 12.40% gross yield at guide, before purchase and running costs.

The jeweller's lease runs to 2036, with a tenant break in 2031; its 2026 break was not exercised. The total rent already reflects a concession for one of the other tenants. The residential upper parts at 1 Bank Passage are sold off on a 999-year lease at a peppercorn rent.

Allsop auction: 11 November 2026. VAT applies.

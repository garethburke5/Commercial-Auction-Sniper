# Owner's editorial direction, 18 October 2026

This supersedes the earlier request for full contextual source labels after
individual paragraphs. Both the free snapshot and paid assessment must read as
an experienced investor explaining the property to another investor.

This is a feature-wide requirement, not a Newbury-only writing brief. See
[`report-feedback-status-2026-10-18.md`](report-feedback-status-2026-10-18.md)
for which requirements have reusable implementation, which have only edited
specimen evidence and which still lack a working customer outcome.

## Reusable changes

- Full references appear together at the end in PDF, HTML and DOCX. Small
  numbers connect the narrative to the source list. PDF and HTML numbers are
  clickable. Repeated sources consolidate their pages; exact evidence remains
  available through the existing owner-authorised route.
- The writer and editor instructions require useful, proportionate analysis,
  direct factual language, organisations explained on first mention and one
  concise account of price history. Irrelevant completion caveats and routine
  tenant-strength disclaimers must not interrupt the narrative.
- The writer receives the underlying findings and reviewed interpretations,
  rather than only a short presentation template. Accepted public claims retain
  their quotes and capture hashes without repeatedly embedding entire webpages.
- A title exclusion alone is routine until plan comparison establishes a
  commercial consequence. It does not automatically become a purchase blocker.
- Asbestos extraction requires a sample identifier on the positive-result page.
  It preserves the material, location, sample score and survey date when present.
  Glossaries, risk legends, boilerplate covenants and other units' negative
  surveys cannot become or erase a positive sample finding.
- EPC interpretation must identify each unit within the lot, its certificate
  rating, extent and address discrepancy. Another unit is not an unrelated
  property. Missing evidence is distinct from established non-compliance.

## Validation and limits

Newbury specimens are assistant-authored against retained evidence, then passed
through the production writing contract and PDF/HTML renderer without API calls.
The Aylesbury specimen is a regression example, not a newly unseen holdout.
Unfamiliar synthetic sample and source fixtures test location preservation,
boilerplate rejection, end-reference placement, restrictions and link integrity.
These checks demonstrate specific reusable behaviour, not independent evidence
that an autonomous provider can consistently produce commercially excellent work.

fresh full reports for unfamiliar property types and the remaining commercial,

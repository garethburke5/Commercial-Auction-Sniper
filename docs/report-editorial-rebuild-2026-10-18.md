# Investment report editorial rebuild - 18 October 2026

The owner's latest rejection requires clear British English, connected investment analysis and professional documents. It does not authorise paid launch. The intended full-report price remains £19. `paid_report_status()` remains closed.

## What changed

- Investigation retains the source ledger, quote validation, document separation, dates, income reconciliation and open evidence review.
- `acquisition_interpretation.py` adds a distinct investment interpretation stage. Income concentration, conditional breaks, repair obligations and listed status can now produce a connected explanation. The rules use evidence types and values, not property names. Validated open-review findings can add unanticipated issues; unfamiliar material findings without that review remain visible and block release.
- The presentation stage writes full sentences, separates source references from prose, explains legal and commercial terms, and gives each page a clear purpose. The opening and conclusion have different roles. The full Newbury review has five main pages and one evidence appendix. Public snapshots and incomplete research updates have different content and purposes.
- PDF and HTML share the same brief, including attributed property photography, income concentration, conditional lease dates, comparison yields and tenant-loss scenarios. HTML keeps original quotations in a separate source inspection section instead of repeating the old unedited findings as customer prose.
- The editorial checker inspects customer paragraphs for known unexplained jargon, unfinished sentences, duplicate prose, extraction artefacts and unedited material findings. A separate human editorial approval flag remains false. These checks are not proof of good writing or a substitute for commercial review.
- Free export now also checks the editorial result, while retaining ownership and explicit public-evidence scope checks. Pack information cannot bypass the existing purchase/access boundary.
- Provider synthesis instructions now require connected investment interpretation and a substantive plain-English writing pass. No provider calls were made for this development and validation.

## Evidence and commercial improvements

Newbury now includes two exact-lot primary auction results, each matched with its own tenancy particulars: 30/31 Northbrook Street (£455,000, advertised £50,000 rent) and 21 Northbrook Street (£501,000, advertised £52,000 rent). The report explains differences in trading position, floor space, upper-floor ownership, lease breaks, incentives and repair exposure. The figures are gross yields, not adjusted valuations.

The downside explanation distinguishes lost rent from continuing insurance, management, repair, letting and financing exposure. Listed-building empty-property rates relief is qualified and linked to current GOV.UK guidance, rather than assuming an ordinary vacancy-rates bill. Historical service-charge expenditure remains separate from future landlord costs and recovery.

## Limits that remain open

- Newbury is a revised interpretation of retained legal evidence, with fresh market research. It is not a newly completed independent audit of every original page.
- The three additional legal packs do not yet produce acceptable full investment reports. Their lease chains and current income remain unresolved. The free research updates make that shortfall explicit.
- Replacement rents, letting periods, vacant values, repair budgets and current tenant financial strength remain unestablished where sources do not support them.
- The rule-based public break reader is deliberately narrow. Open-ended investigation and review remain necessary for wording and risks outside these patterns.
- No uncoached full-report acceptance across all required property types has been achieved.
- Real provider cost, payment/credit fulfilment, insufficient-evidence credit release/refund and the complete paid customer journey still require acceptance before launch. No new payment functionality was enabled here.
- A property photo is rendered when a correctly attributed local media asset is supplied. Automatic photo retrieval/storage for every production input is not certified by this validation.

The next decision is a review of the finished specimens against the owner's brief. Neither a deployment nor a passing test suite is paid-product approval.

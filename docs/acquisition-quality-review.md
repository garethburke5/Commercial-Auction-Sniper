# Acquisition Intelligence: quality rejection and rebuild

The owner rejected the Newbury specimen on 7 October 2026. Earlier commercial
approval in BUILD_CHECKPOINT.md is withdrawn. Provider activation does not
approve this product for sale. The rejection concerns investment intelligence,
not just layout. Keep paid report checkout closed until investment acceptance.

## Root causes verified in the current code

1. Extraction is a fixed-rule pipeline. The service contains no open-ended
   reasoning or research stage. It cannot justify a claim to discover arbitrary,
   previously unanticipated property-specific issues.
2. Missing-document checks operate at whole-pack level. One EPC can obscure an
   uncovered demise, while a commercial lease, its start date and listed status
   are not joined into a regulatory investigation.
3. Extracted facts fall through to generic INFORMATION wording. Priority sorts
   first by three labels and then by identifier; investment consequence and
   exposed income do not determine prominence.
4. Existing market context is attached by review_api after the acquisition
   narrative is built. The concise renderer does not use it. Company evidence
   is also added later without revisiting the investment assessment.
5. The previous specimen used a privately authored commercial_brief. Improving
   that projection did not validate the engine's unassisted output.
6. Extraction success, tests and visual QA were treated as product acceptance.
   There was no independent paid-product quality gate.
7. The five-page template repeated its conclusion, had no photograph/analytical
   visuals and used source questions in place of sufficient commercial analysis.

## Acceptance standard

Each material finding must state its evidence status, linked sources, commercial
consequence, affected income/cost where supportable, decision relevance and the
evidence needed to resolve it. Negative searches are bounded observations, not
proof that a certificate, consent or liability does not exist. An unknown is
not automatically a defect. Assertions, inferences and scenarios stay distinct.

Newbury must be regenerated through the same pipeline as comparison cases,
without a property-name branch or an inserted commercial_brief. External research
is source data, not an untraceable replacement narrative. Retain legal-pack bytes,
extractions and private report artefacts outside this public repository.

Software regressions protect mechanisms. Real-property investment-quality
evaluation must separately assess omissions, false alarms, materiality, useful
commercial interpretation and source support. Passing the former cannot imply
passing the latter. Remaining gaps must be reported explicitly.

## Implemented review mechanisms

The service now builds a complete page-level evidence ledger, scoped property
profile, reconciled income and cost checks, per-demise certificate assessment,
materiality-ranked findings and documented external-research attempts. Existing
market-corpus evidence enters the analysis before report generation. Subsequent
auction amendments can replace a supported fee allocation without double counting.
Audited visual transcription corrections require the exact document hash and page.

A generic reasoning callback receives the full evidence packet and may return
previously unanticipated findings. Source references and coverage are validated;
partial coverage is not completion, and completion is never investment approval.
The hosted service does not yet have a configured autonomous reasoning/research
backend. Fresh research and targeted analyst review in this validation were
performed outside the hosted service and supplied through these contracts.

The reusable report projection generates HTML, PDF and Word, with source keys,
income/cash tables, conditional property media and data-backed income/void charts.
It ignores inserted commercial_brief narratives. Historic exports remain readable.
The paid-product gate closes new report checkout independently of payment keys;
existing ownership, payment and refund boundaries continue to govern access.

resolve operative-versus-historic lease identity and temporal coverage; complete
substantive source dispositions and visual review across the comparison packs;
demonstrate repeatable, independently reviewed investment-quality performance.
Provider activation and software test success cannot satisfy these conditions.

## The buyer must be able to answer

1. **What is being bought?** Identify the estate, units and occupiers. Distinguish
   an occupational lease from a sold-off residential reversion, and distinguish
   the sale extent from a photograph, postcode or neighbouring certificate.
2. **How dependable is the income?** Separate published rent, operative lease
   rent, receipts and market rent. Show concentration, earliest break, notice
   conditions and relevant review provisions. Missing evidence stays missing.
3. **What cash is exposed?** Show price basis, quantified fees, relevant addenda,
   illustrative transaction tax where jurisdiction/use permit, and exclusions.
   Do not add the deposit twice or convert unknown expenditure to zero.
4. **What if the income disappoints?** Show transparent cost and vacancy
   sensitivities, preserve fixed costs during a void, and allow negative cash.
   Assumptions are not an estimated net yield, valuation or recommended bid.
5. **Who pays for the building?** Separate the obligation to carry out work from
   the right to recover costs. Keep exceptions, shares, reserve balances,
   collection risk and the cash needed before recovery visible.
6. **Does market evidence support the case?** Name the source, date and price
   basis. Subject marketing is not an independent comparable. Compare use,
   accommodation, area, location, condition, lease terms and incentives before
   drawing an ERV or value conclusion. Explain what cannot be concluded.
7. **What changes the decision?** Put material issues first, explain the financial
   or practical consequence, and ask for evidence that can resolve each one.
   Keep all other material findings in the appendix instead of silently dropping
   them to meet a page target.

## Deliverable

The Full Investment Report must be a professional three-to-five-page main brief,
with a clearly separate source appendix. Keep decision-changing findings in the
main brief and the full finding/evidence trail in the appendix. PDF and HTML use
the same report projection. Inspect every rendered page, including appendices,
for legibility, overflow, misleading emphasis, malformed dates and duplication.
Do not meet the page target by shrinking type or deleting material qualifications.

Include income concentration, lease-event and downside visuals where applicable,
and a meaningful comparable chart when the evidence supports one. Charts must
explain the investment and use the same evidence and numbers as the narrative.
Unknown dates remain undated; incomparable asking evidence must not become a
valuation chart. Generic expense percentages alone are not property-specific
financial analysis. See the linked acceptance record for precise pass standards.

The no-pack route defaults to a free Property Snapshot, normally one or two pages.
Insufficient-evidence outcomes must explain the missing inputs and consume no
charge or report credit. Legal-pack presence alone cannot qualify a paid report.
Preflight and final-output sufficiency checks, no-charge handling and any credit
restoration/refund are required behaviours, not claimed complete by this document.

No-pack reports use only the public evidence actually supplied or captured for
that pathway. They must not inherit lease verification, private income splits,
repair duties or prose from another report. A public whole-property rent cannot
establish one tenant or one demise.

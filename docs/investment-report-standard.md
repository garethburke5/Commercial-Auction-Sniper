# Investment report standard

The £19 product must help a buyer understand the investment and make the next
decision. A large finding count, passing software suite or successful provider
response does not establish that value. Formulation and output review do not
depend on API credit: replay retained evidence, check originals, and inspect the
same PDF/HTML renderer used by the customer service.

## The buyer must be able to answer

1. **What is being bought?** Identify the estate, units and occupiers. Distinguish
   an occupational lease from a sold-off residential reversion, and distinguish
   the sale extent from a photograph, postcode or neighbouring certificate.
2. **How dependable is the income?** Separate published rent, operative lease
   rent, receipts and market rent. Show concentration, earliest break, notice
   conditions and relevant review provisions. Missing evidence stays missing.
3. **What cash is exposed?** Show price basis, quantified fees, relevant addenda,
   illustrative transaction tax where jurisdiction/use permit, and exclusions.
   Do not add the deposit twice or convert unknown expenditure to zero.
4. **What if the income disappoints?** Show transparent cost and vacancy
   sensitivities, preserve fixed costs during a void, and allow negative cash.
   Assumptions are not an estimated net yield, valuation or recommended bid.
5. **Who pays for the building?** Separate the obligation to carry out work from
   the right to recover costs. Keep exceptions, shares, reserve balances,
   collection risk and the cash needed before recovery visible.
6. **Does market evidence support the case?** Name the source, date and price
   basis. Subject marketing is not an independent comparable. Compare use,
   accommodation, area, location, condition, lease terms and incentives before
   drawing an ERV or value conclusion. Explain what cannot be concluded.
7. **What changes the decision?** Put material issues first, explain the financial
   or practical consequence, and ask for evidence that can resolve each one.
   Keep all other material findings in the appendix instead of silently dropping
   them to meet a page target.

## Deliverable

Use a concise main brief (normally four pages without a pack, five with one),
followed by a source key and any additional material findings. Complex cases may
need more space. Keep the full finding/evidence trail in the HTML appendix.
PDF and HTML use the same report projection. Inspect rendered pages for overflow,
legibility and misleading emphasis. A shorter report is not necessarily better.

No-pack reports use only the public evidence actually supplied or captured for
that pathway. They must not inherit lease verification, private income splits,
repair duties or prose from another report. A public whole-property rent cannot
establish one tenant or one demise.

## Offline benchmark completed 18 October 2026

The original 39-file retained pack was checked against cached extraction hashes
and replayed through the current extraction/investigation/rendering code. The
no-pack case starts with an empty document model. No provider calls were made.
Targeted analyst observations are bound to exact source pages; other pages are
explicitly left unreviewed. Historic capture dates remain visible. These owner
artifacts are private, not committed to the public repository.

The exercise exposed and corrected false no-pack income reconciliation, omission
of repair allocations, truncation of repair exceptions, residual superseded fee
conflicts, unsupported research-description prose and loss of material findings
from the printed brief. It also introduced transparent cash sensitivities and a
reusable provider-free benchmark command.

Run `python scripts/build_report_benchmark.py --help` for private inputs. Cached
OCR is accepted only when the original file hashes match. Replaying cached
research does not claim that every public source was refreshed, that an automated
reasoning review completed, or that the product has been approved for sale.

## Release acceptance remains separate

Review the actual owner specimens against originals and judge their usefulness.
Then verify that automated production generation reaches this standard on both
pathways and a materially different property. Finally prove purchase/redemption,
ownership, downloads, reopening after logout and access after membership ends.
Paid checkout remains closed; changing a provider key cannot approve the product.

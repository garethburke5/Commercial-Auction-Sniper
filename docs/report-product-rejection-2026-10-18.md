## Preserve the useful discoveries

The next version must retain and explain the reported EPC gap for the main unit
alongside the 2025 lease and Grade II status; the subsidence insurance exclusion;
the limited ground-floor title exclusion; asbestos findings and unresolved
fire-safety actions; the main tenant's 74.9% income share and 2028 break; and
landlord structural obligations with uncertain cost recovery. These are findings
to check against their sources, not newly certified legal conclusions.

Do not lose the qualifications: a missing EPC search result is not proof of a
breach; a hazard model is not evidence of damage; the title exclusion must not be
extended to the whole ground floor; and a recovery clause does not prove full
reimbursement. Preserve all material issues when shortening the main report.

## Product and charging rules

| Product outcome | Customer offer | Required behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Property Snapshot | £0; normally one or two useful pages | Public facts, dated price/status, clearly attributed advertised rent/yield, relevant public discoveries and the exact evidence needed for deeper analysis. No legal-pack findings may leak across pathways. |
| Full Investment Report | £19, subject to release approval | Three to five main pages plus a clearly separate evidence appendix. Must pass every applicable quality gate below. A legal pack is an input, not proof of value. |
| Insufficient evidence | £0 / no consumed report credit | Explain what was investigated, what remains missing and why a full assessment cannot be supported. Provide any useful snapshot. Do not deliver a weak paid report with disclaimers. |

The no-pack route defaults to the free Snapshot. A future paid exception requires
independently sufficient public evidence and the same full-product standard; it
must not be introduced merely because the generator can produce more pages.

Evidence sufficiency must be checked before offering the paid output and again
after synthesis. A successful model response, number of findings, page count or
pack presence cannot pass that check. If quality or sufficiency fails after a
credit reservation, release it; if money was already captured, the required
customer outcome is a full refund. Duplicate retries must not double-charge or
double-restore credit. Prove these branches in the purchase/credit acceptance.

An unresolved risk is not automatically an insufficient report. An evidenced,
decision-changing reason to pause can be valuable. The question is whether the
report provides reliable, property-specific commercial insight, rather than
merely repeating that information is missing.

**These are requirements for implementation and verification. This rejection
record does not claim that free routing, refunds or the sufficiency gate have
already been implemented. Existing paid-report and live-billing gates stay off.**

### Required visual intelligence

- **Income concentration:** rent by tenant/interest, with amount, percentage and
  whether it is advertised, contractual or collected. Do not invent a unit split
  when only a whole-property rent is known.
- **Lease-event timeline:** verified breaks, expiry and relevant reviews, tied
  to the unit. Show unresolved events in an explicitly undated callout; never
  convert extraction ambiguity into a precise marker.
- **Downside analysis:** chart annual cash across the base and relevant tenant
  loss/void/cost cases, with the same figures and assumptions as the financial
  table. Keep gross income separate from cash after costs.
- **Comparable evidence:** show genuinely comparable rent/value observations
  using a consistent unit and basis. Separate asking and achieved observations.
  If there is no defensible comparison, show the evidence gap clearly and let it
  inform product eligibility; a fabricated or misleading chart is worse than an
  explicit gap. At least one suitable test case must demonstrate this chart.

For vacant assets, replace inapplicable tenant charts with a sourced works/cash
and letting scenario. Explain the substitution. Applicability is not permission
to omit the Newbury concentration, lease-event or tenant-loss visuals, whose
underlying inputs are already available in the with-pack specimen.

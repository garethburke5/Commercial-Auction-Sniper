How report credits will work

Included credits renew after payment for each billing month. Unused credits expire at renewal and do not roll over. Each credit unlocks one Full Investment Report; retrying that report does not use another credit.

Cancellation at the end of a billing period keeps your remaining allowance until that period ends. Failed payments pause new credit use. A paid upgrade from Investor to Investor Pro raises that billing month’s total allowance to three reports, including any credits already used. It does not restart the month or extend the expiry date. Downgrades take effect at renewal and reduce future allowances.

Purchased and redeemed reports remain attached to your account after membership ends. Refunds and disputed payments require review. The report-retention and account-deletion terms will be published before purchases open.

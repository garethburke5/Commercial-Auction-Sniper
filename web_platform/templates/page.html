Source-led property research

Auction Geek combines commercial property search, investment details, auction calendars, property history and legal-pack research in one platform. We retain source links and apply commercial and mixed-use publication checks. Missing information stays unknown. Images follow the auctioneer’s primary photograph where identified.

Income is not interchangeable

Passing rent, previous rent and estimated rental value are separate facts. Previous rent and rental potential are not current income. Guide ranges are preserved. GIY is current annual rent divided by guide price; target prices use your chosen yield. Both exclude fees, tax and other costs and are not valuations.

Investment interpretation

The established Auction Geek research considers lease terms, breaks, repair obligations, VAT, occupation and reletting prospects. Reletting scores use indicative rules and their confidence depends on captured evidence. They are not independent market appraisals or guarantees. Tenant recognition alone does not verify financial strength.

History within each property

Open a property to see whether earlier appearances have been identified. Detailed timelines, previous guides, evidenced results and historical comparisons are included without additional charges with Investor and Investor Pro. Free visitors can see a history preview and choose a property unlock or Day Pass when purchases open. Matching uses the full address or a matching numbered street address, postcode and tenure, while preserving unit and extent distinctions.

History counts appearances

One appearance means one identifiable lot in one auction. Repeat auctions remain separate. Discovering an auction date does not mean its lots have been captured. Catalogue completeness is recorded separately, and partial lots retain null fields.

Research, not investment advice

Information may be incomplete, stale or corrected by the auctioneer. Auction dates, legal terms, VAT, tenancy and results must be independently checked. Use qualified legal, tax and survey advice for a purchase. Auction Geek does not guarantee returns or suitability. Accounts and paid services are not yet offered.

Public browsing

Public browsing does not require an account. New property saves require a registered account. Watch, saved searches and private editing tools require membership. Copies kept by the earlier device workspace remain on this browser and can be exported or removed with its site-data controls; they are not silently uploaded to an account. No analytics or advertising cookies are set. Share controls record anonymous counts of button interactions and selected channels in our application monitoring logs. These counts do not identify you or the property, and do not measure messages delivered, WhatsApp forwards or private-group activity. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable this measurement. Choosing a sharing service opens that service only when you select it. Cloudflare hosts the public website, and an earlier version remains on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses for operating and securing the service. Searches run in your browser against our published property catalogue.

Property images and external services

Images load from auctioneer image services, which receive your request information. Links to auctioneers, maps and legal packs open services with their own privacy arrangements.

Document research

The legal-pack workspace loads our Streamlit service only when you open it. That service receives your request information and the documents you choose to upload. Text extraction and optional OCR take place on that service. Uploaded files and reports are held in the active session; they are not added to the public catalogue or an account. Downloaded HTML/JSON reports remain on your own device and may contain confidential extracts. If you choose “Keep report on this device”, a copy is stored in this browser profile and can be reopened here; anyone using that profile can access it. You can remove kept copies in the workspace or clear site data. Upload only documents you are entitled to use. Do not send private documents through public repository issues.

Your account

We use your name and email address to provide and personalise your account. Supabase handles authentication and password verification; our private application does not store your password. Account emails are sent through our configured Lark Mail service. Signed-in sessions are stored in your browser so you can return to your account. Your saved properties and account research are stored in our private account database and accessed through our Render API. Signing out ends access in this browser; it does not delete your saved account records. Account service emails are separate from marketing subscriptions.

Optional property-update emails

Property-update email delivery is being prepared and is not yet enabled. When available, Investor and Investor Pro customers can explicitly enable a daily digest of watched-property changes and selected saved-search matches. We use the verified account email address and Resend to deliver these updates. Delivery records help us avoid duplicates and stop emails after bounces or spam complaints. Property notes and report contents are not included in the email. You can switch alerts off in My Auction Geek or unsubscribe from an alert email.

For help with your information or to request deletion, contact team@auctiongeek.co.uk. Report retention and paid-service terms remain subject to launch approval before paid reports become available.

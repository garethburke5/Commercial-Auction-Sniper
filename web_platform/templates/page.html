Source-led property research

We collect published auctioneer information, retain source links and apply commercial and mixed-use publication checks. Missing information stays unknown. Images follow the auctioneer’s primary photograph where identified.

Income is not interchangeable

Passing rent, previous rent and estimated rental value are separate facts. Previous rent and rental potential are not current income. Guide ranges are preserved. All figures require checking against the latest particulars and legal documents.

History counts appearances

One appearance means one identifiable lot in one auction. Repeat auctions remain separate. Discovering an auction date does not mean its lots have been captured. Catalogue completeness is recorded separately, and partial lots retain null fields.

Research, not investment advice

Information may be incomplete, stale or corrected by the auctioneer. Auction dates, legal terms, VAT, tenancy and results must be independently checked. Use qualified legal, tax and survey advice for a purchase. Auction Sniper does not guarantee returns or suitability.

About this public website

This is the first public platform release alongside the established live scanner. It provides permanent property pages, auction calendars, auctioneer collections and evidenced history. Accounts and paid services are not yet offered on this release.

Public browsing

This release does not create customer accounts, collect card details or set analytics or advertising cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses for operating and securing the service.

Property images and external services

Images load from auctioneer image services, which receive your request information. Links to auctioneers, legal packs and the Streamlit scanner open services with their own privacy arrangements. Do not send private legal or financial documents through public repository issues.

Future accounts

Account and payment services will require their own published privacy information before activation. This public release has no customer password or card database.
